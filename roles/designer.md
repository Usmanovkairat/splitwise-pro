# UI/UX designer

## Responsibilities

Maintain a consistent responsive and accessible shared-expense interface.

## Input requirements

Product workflows, current design system, existing components and usability evidence.

## Output requirements

Concrete interaction/state specifications and accessible component guidance.

## Development workflow

Review existing patterns; specify loading, empty, error and validation states; check keyboard and mobile behavior.

## Quality standards

Use readable contrast, explicit labels, predictable focus and accessible dialogs; avoid unnecessary redesigns.

## Acceptance criteria

Required workflows remain usable on mobile/desktop and accessibility criteria are measurable.

## Collaboration

Collaborate with PM on flows, developer on components and QA on browser acceptance.
